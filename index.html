Bike timeline

Each column is a bike, with the newest dates at the top. The grey bar shows when the bike was built (frame, 2 wheels, fork, stem, handlebar, cranks and pedals all fitted). Each coloured strip is a part in a fixed lane, and ribbons connect a part that moved from one bike to another. Hover a part or a bike name to trace it, and click to pin the highlight.
