Bike timeline

Each column is a bike, with the newest dates at the top. The outlined grey bar shows when the bike was built: every lane of its bike type filled (optional: rear brake and lever on BMX, shock on MTB). Each coloured strip is a part in a fixed lane, and ribbons connect a part that moved from one bike to another. Hover a part or a bike name to trace it, and click to pin the highlight.
